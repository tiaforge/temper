Overlays dialogs, sheets (sheet.css), menus (menu.css)

Dialogs

A tall dialog keeps its header and footer in view and scrolls the content between them. The page behind an open modal dialog does not scroll.

Sheets

A dialog with data-tm-sheet slides in from an edge: end and start are full-height side panels, bottom runs along the bottom edge, narrow is a bottom sheet on phones and an ordinary dialog on wider screens.

Menus and popovers

Popovers and menus fade in and out like dialogs. A data-tm-caption labels a group of items; a kbd at the end of an item is its shortcut.

Terms of service

Please read these terms before you continue. The header and the buttons stay where they are while this text scrolls.

1. Accounts

You are responsible for the activity on your account and for keeping your password safe. Tell us at once if you notice access you did not allow.

2. Your content

You keep the rights to what you upload. You give us the permission we need to store it, back it up and show it to the people you share it with.

3. Calendars and invitations

Invitations you send go out from your address. Guests see the event title, the time and the other guests unless you hide the guest list.

4. Availability

We aim for the service to be available at all times but cannot promise it. Planned maintenance is announced a week ahead.

5. Ending the service

You can export your data and close your account at any time. We keep backups for 30 days after that, then delete them.

6. Changes

When these terms change we tell you a month ahead, by mail and in the app.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms go to the address in the imprint.
