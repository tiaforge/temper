The parts, and when to use them

wash Working views and front doors: colour at the top that fades within 18rem, plain where people work (this page's top and both examples).
mesh A whole surface of colour: a marketing section or an empty state, rarely more than one per page.
glass Over colour or imagery only (on a plain page it is just a grey card).
gradient Small accents: a banner, a highlighted tile. Not a slab behind content.
